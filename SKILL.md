## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a future-archaeology specimen, not a pixel copy of the photograph. Compress the subject into a life-symbol on a regular grid: limited pixel units, modular rhythm and local variation. Keep one small subject, extreme white space and an archival balance. Delete background and unrelated objects. Reject a detailed pixel replica, a filled environment and a decorative mosaic.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
